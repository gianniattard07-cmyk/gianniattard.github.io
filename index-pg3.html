Institute of Applied Sciences

At the Institute of Applied Sciences, learners are prepared for technological careers within the health, pharmaceutical, environmental, and food and beverage sectors. Students learn to appreciate the fundamental principles of science related to technological operations at the workplace while developing the skills to work in laboratories, hospitals, farms, and with environmental technologies and methodologies. The institute also caters to Agriculture, Aquatics, and Animal Sciences students. The facilities for these Agriculture courses are based at our campus in Qormi.
